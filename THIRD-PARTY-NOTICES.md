## Source-to-output contract

Every externally informed source record carries material identifiers resolving
to `docs/used-materials.md`, plus a specific locator and description of the
adaptation. Every authored approximation is explicitly marked and explained.
Generated review packs retain the corresponding credits, license and source
links, retained notices, and indication of changes. Record inclusion does not
authorize use of other portions or separately licensed figures. In particular,
permission-only figures in Hands-on Anatomy are excluded.

## Whole-body mass approximation for squat R3

Pawel Kudzia, Erika Jackson and Genevieve Dumas (2022), *Estimating body segment
parameters from three-dimensional human body scans*, PLOS ONE 17(1): e0262296.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0262296 . Copyright 2022 Kudzia et al.
Original Proposed columns of Tables 3 and 6 are licensed CC BY 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . The project blends male/female
mean mass weights, normalizes its 16-region budget and adapts selected limb
COM fractions to its authored skeleton. Head/trunk/pelvis/foot mass-point
placements are project approximations. No images, scans, source code, other
comparison columns or density tables are reproduced. This adaptation is for
static plausibility only and does not imply the authors endorse this model.

Satoshi Kasahara, Tomoya Ishida, Jiang Linjing, Ami Chiba, Mina Samukawa and
Harukazu Tohyama (2024), *Relationship among the COM Motion, the Lower Extremity
and the Trunk during the Squat*, Journal of Human Kinetics 93:29–39.
https://doi.org/10.5114/jhk/183066 . Copyright 2024 Academy of Physical Education
in Katowice. CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Discussion
and conclusions inform coordinated movement qualitatively. Numerical targets
are project-authored; no experimental angle tables, figures or motion records
are copied. No endorsement is implied. See docs/used-materials.md for clearance.
